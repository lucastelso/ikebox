# Code of conduct

**[Português](docs/pt-BR/codigo-de-conduta.md)** · English

Ikebox is a small project made in the open. Everyone who takes part,
in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in its name, is
expected to make it a welcoming, harassment-free place, whatever their
experience, background, identity or language (English and Portuguese are
both fine here).

## Expected

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Disagree with ideas, not people; give and take feedback gracefully.
- Respect people's privacy. In this project that also means: never post
  anyone's credentials, gateway addresses or internal network names, and
  remove them when you see them (the app's "Copy anonymized" helps).
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualized
  language or imagery.
- Trolling and deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting

Report privately through GitHub's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/lucastelso/ikebox/security/advisories/new),
starting the title with **"Conduct:"** (it reaches only the maintainers, not
the public). Say what happened, where (links), and when. Reports are read by
the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues
that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
someone, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is. Whoever
is reported is heard before a ban, unless that would put someone at risk.
